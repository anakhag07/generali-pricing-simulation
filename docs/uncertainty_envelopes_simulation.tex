\section{Simulation Instantiation for Linear $f$}

We use contexts $x\in\mathbb R^3$, scalar actions $a\in\mathbb R$, and
$n=60$ calibration observations. Independently across observations and between
streams, we draw
\[
 x_i\sim\mathcal N(0,I_3),\qquad a_i\sim\mathcal N(0,1),\qquad
 \varepsilon_i\sim\mathcal N(0,1),\qquad
 y_i=f(x_i,a_i)+\varepsilon_i.
\]
The true response and frozen predictor are
\begin{align*}
 f(x,a)&=x_1-0.5x_2+0.25x_3+2a,\\
 \widehat f(x,a)&=0.2+0.8x_1-0.4x_2+0.5x_3+1.7a.
\end{align*}
The predictor is hand specified: it is not fitted, and there is no separate
predictor-training dataset. Both constructions below share the same saved
calibration dataset. These analytical launches use one realization and do not
redraw calibration responses.

For the auxiliary fit, let $\phi(x,a)=(1,x_1,x_2,x_3,a)^\top$, so $p=5$.
The design $P\in\mathbb R^{60\times5}$ has rows $\phi(x_i,a_i)^\top$;
its full column rank is checked before fitting. With $Q=P^\top P$, ordinary
least squares gives $\widetilde\beta=Q^{-1}P^\top y$ and
$\widetilde f(x,a)=\phi(x,a)^\top\widetilde\beta$; the implementation uses
QR and triangular solves. Taking the known noise bound $\sigma=1$ and
$\delta=0.05$, the two radii and their envelopes are
\begin{align*}
 r_{\mathrm{const}}(x,a)&=3,\\
 w_\delta(x,a)&=\sigma\sqrt{p/\delta}
       \sqrt{\phi(x,a)^\top Q^{-1}\phi(x,a)}
       =10\sqrt{\phi(x,a)^\top Q^{-1}\phi(x,a)},\\
 r_\delta(x,a)&=|\widehat f(x,a)-\widetilde f(x,a)|+w_\delta(x,a),\\
 E_r(x,a)&=[\widehat f(x,a)-r(x,a),\ \widehat f(x,a)+r(x,a)].
\end{align*}
Thus the constant construction has full width $6$. No residual estimate
replaces $\sigma$ in the analytical construction.

Coverage means simultaneous containment at the observed contexts and every
real action:
\[
 \mathcal C_r=\{f(x_i,a)\in E_r(x_i,a)
                 \text{ for all }i=1,\ldots,60\text{ and }a\in\mathbb R\}.
\]
The exact-arithmetic moment bound gives $\Pr(\mathcal C_{r_\delta}\mid P)\ge0.95$.
This is a sampling-probability lower bound, distinct from the realized
containment decision for the saved dataset. Dimension enters through $p$ and
the design, without an additional context/action union bound. The all-real
checker uses exact rational polynomial signs for stored numerical coefficients;
it does not enclose floating-point fitting or evaluation error. The constant radius fails
on $\mathbb R$ because the predictor and truth have different action slopes,
even when it covers the displayed bounded window.

Tightness is measured by $r(x,a)$ and signed excess
$s_r(x,a)=r(x,a)-|\widehat f(x,a)-f(x,a)|$, retaining negative values.
Means and inverse-empirical-CDF quantiles at $0.5$ and $0.9$ give equal weight
to all $60\times101$ pairs $(x_i,-2+4j/100)$, $j=0,\ldots,100$.
This finite diagnostic measure is separate from both the Gaussian logging
law and the all-real coverage target. For the context-adjusted display only,
subtracting $g(x)=x_1-0.5x_2+0.25x_3$ leaves the common truth $2a$;
saved radii and coverage decisions are unchanged.

The calibration figure shares an action axis on $[-2,2]$ across three panels:
the original adjusted common band, truth, and mean adjusted frozen predictor
with noisy $y_i-g(x_i)$;
the half-width $H(a)=[U(a)-L(a)]/2$ of that same all-context intersection
$[L(a),U(a)]$, masked when empty; and logged-action counts in width-$0.5$ bins
with a rug. It displays 58 of 60 draws; two lie above $2$. Analytical width uses
joint-design leverage, not inverse local action density. The intersection's
half-width is not the mean radius; individual radii and guarantees are unchanged.
Mean radius, width, and discrepancy remain saved diagnostics. No model is refitted.

Seed roots $1101$, $1102$, $1103$, and $1104$ control contexts, logged actions,
observations, and method randomness, respectively. Observation and method
seeds are derived with the repository's \texttt{derive\_seed} using the label
\texttt{repetition:0}. The deterministic linear method consumes no random draws.
